Scrip

Born owing. Dig your way out.

Redeemable at the company store

A solo delve-and-return game narrated by Claude. Everyone in the Verge is born owing a Share of a war nobody is allowed to read about, and on Talus the Combine holds your crew's. Below the camp lie the Deep Shafts, abandoned after the Mutiny. Your crew goes down for what's left, and you decide how they meet what's waiting. Then you bring them back up to heal, trade and get ready to go down again.

How it plays

You give the orders. They have to carry them out.

You play the leader, not the soldier on the ground. Set the approach for each encounter and your crew reacts on their own. You still press to roll the dice, and you still have to watch them land.

1 · Prepare

Choose the crew

Pick who goes down, what they carry and what you can afford to lose.

2 · Delve

Set the approach

Every encounter asks how you'll take it. The crew does the rest, for better or worse.

3 · Return

Count the cost

Injuries, collapses and spent supplies follow you back up. Not every delve pays.

4 · Settle

Between delves

The settlement has its own story, told between runs: rumours, debts, rivals and the next way down.

Made for miniatures

Your painted models can be your crew. The game keeps track of the story, so the table time stays short.

Story over stats

The focus is on who your crew are and what happens to them, not on counting squares.

Same world as Chronicle

Scrip is set on Talus, one of the worlds of Amnesty. It runs in your own Claude app like every Retinue game, and your saves stay in your copy.
