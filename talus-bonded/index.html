Talus Bonded

Born owing. Dig your way out.

Bond No. 4471-T · Transfer prohibited

A solo delve-and-return game narrated by Claude. Everyone in the Verge is born owing a Share of a war nobody is allowed to read about, and on Talus the Combine holds your crew's bonds. Nobody expects them to be paid off, least of all the Combine. Below the camp lie the Deep Shafts, abandoned after the Mutiny. Your crew goes down for what's left, and you decide how they meet what's waiting. Then you bring them back up to heal, trade and fight for a slightly better life on a world that bills you for the air.

The Apportionment

The war was forgiven. The bill was not.

When the Charter War ended, the Amnesty pardoned every crime and sealed every record. It kept every debt. The cost of the fleets, the garrisons and the burning of Cinder was divided among every living colonist and their children, and stamped on their papers at birth as a Share.

The itemised bill is sealed with the rest of the war. People across the Verge are paying for something they are not allowed to read about.

On Talus, the Kessel Fuel Combine buys labourers' Shares and works them off in scrip. Its hold on a person is a bond, and everyone in the camp goes by their bond number. A century of interest has made the numbers so large that nobody alive has seen a labourer clear one.

Talus

A frozen world at the edge of the accounts.

Talus is one of the worlds of Amnesty, and its forgotten edge. Fuel wells flare on the ice, the garrison at Coldharbour has been refused reinforcements twice, and the oldest workings were left to freeze after the Mutiny.

Home

Low Gantry

A scrap camp built on an old headframe, a day's walk past the last patrol. Salvage crews, a struck-off medic, a Combine factor, and a bookie who used to be a shop steward.

Where you work

The Deep Shafts

Frozen galleries, ore lifts, pump halls and bunkhouses from before the Mutiny. Combine strongrooms nobody emptied, and the bodies the ice kept.

Below that

The Melt

Flooded galleries where the ice has gone warm. Something ticks through the rock in a pattern that repeats. Nobody has explained it, and nobody who listens for long wants to.

Forty years ago

Mutiny Field

Where the miners who burned the Share registers were broken. Their strike committee had pooled money to buy their freedom. The fund was never found.

How it plays

You give the orders. They have to carry them out.

You play the leader, not the soldier on the ground. Set the approach for each encounter and your crew reacts on their own. You still press to roll the dice, and you still have to watch them land.

1 · Prepare

Choose the crew

Pick who goes down, what they carry and what you can afford to lose.

2 · Delve

Set the approach

Every encounter asks how you'll take it. The crew does the rest, for better or worse.

3 · Return

Count the cost

Injuries, collapses and spent supplies follow you back up. Not every delve pays.

4 · Settle

Between delves

The settlement has its own story, told between runs: rumours, debts, rivals and the next way down.

Miniatures optional

Your painted models can stand in for your crew if you like. The game keeps track of the story either way, so table time stays short.

Story over stats

The focus is on who your crew are and what happens to them, not on counting squares.

Same world as Chronicle

Talus Bonded is set on Talus, one of the worlds of Amnesty. It runs in your own Claude app like every Retinue game, and your saves stay in your copy.

What you can win

Not freedom. A better life.

The bond will never be paid. What your crew can fight for is everything that makes Talus less cruel: a bunk, a medic, a say in which shaft they go down. There are two ways to get it, and they pull against each other.

Climb the company

The ladder

Meet your tonnage, find someone to vouch for you, keep the Combine's opinion high, and sit the review. Every grade buys a right you can feel.

  1. ContractorPaid per haul. Docked when injured. Replaceable.

  3. Employee Season 1Company medical and a bunk. Your bond dies with you, not passed to kin.
  4. Rated GangerYou choose the shaft.

Build the strike

The fund

Forty years on, nobody thinks the Mutiny's strike fund would free anyone. It could pay for a strike: food, medicine and bribes while the shafts stand idle. Somewhere below, the trail to it is still there.

  • Skim the haul for the cause, and slow your own promotion.
  • Cover another crew's shortfall, and earn the camp's trust.
  • Ask the wrong questions, and Combine security starts turning up in the shafts.
  • Get caught, and you're a Contractor again.

Season 1 ends with a choice. Your Employee review falls due about the time the fund comes within reach. Sign the contract, or hand everything to the camp. Either way, you live with what happens.
